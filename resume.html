DeepResearch RewardBench: Evaluating Reward Models for Deep Research Reports 2025 – 2026

Co-first author · Accepted to Findings of EMNLP 2026.共同第一作者 · 已被 Findings of EMNLP 2026 接收。

  • Built a reward-model benchmark for long-form deep-research reports, with an end-to-end pipeline spanning 19 agent/LLM families; combined automatic filtering and human verification to construct preference data covering 331 tasks and 866 reports.
  • Evaluated 17 models from 7B to 235B with Best-of-N selection and full-ranking consistency metrics; analyzed URL robustness, input-order bias, and comparative versus pointwise scoring.
  • Developed a four-category judge-error taxonomy and a few-shot classifier reaching ~87% agreement with human annotation.
  • 构建面向长篇深度研究报告的奖励模型评测基准，设计覆盖 19 个智能体/大模型家族的端到端流水线，结合自动过滤与人工校验，产出包含 331 项任务、866 份报告的偏好数据。
  • 基于 Best-of-N 选择与全排序一致性指标评测 17 个 7B–235B 模型,并分析 URL 鲁棒性、输入顺序偏差及对比式与打分式范式。
  • 构建四类裁判错误体系及 few-shot 自动分类器,与人工标注一致率约 87%。
LongJudgeBench: LLM Judge Evaluation of Long-Range Agent Trajectories 2026

First author · Manuscript in preparation: planned submission to COLING in October 2026.第一作者 · 论文准备中：计划于 2026 年 10 月投稿 COLING。

  • Designed and led a benchmark testing whether LLM judges can detect errors, locate them, and cite supporting evidence in long agent trajectories. Combined real error trajectories from APB, AgentHallu, and AppWorld with controlled trajectory pairs to measure how evidence distance affects judging.
  • Built an error-injection pipeline based on real failure types, such as wrong parameters, fabricated entities, and API misuse. Each pair differs by one injected step, with all observations produced by actual simulator execution; evaluated hidden inconsistencies separately from explicit exceptions.
  • Found that judges could still detect errors as evidence became more distant, but were less able to cite the right evidence. Memory probes traced this gap to failure to revisit evidence rather than context capacity; requiring explicit citations helped, showing why detection scores alone are insufficient.
  • 设计并主导评测基准，检验 LLM 判官能否在长程智能体轨迹中发现错误、定位错误并找出证据。结合 APB、AgentHallu 与 AppWorld 的真实错误轨迹和受控配对轨迹，测量证据与错误之间的距离如何影响判断。
  • 根据参数错值、虚构实体、API 误用等真实失败类型构建错误注入流水线。每对轨迹仅有一个注入步骤不同，所有观测均来自模拟器真实执行；将隐藏的不一致与直接抛出的异常分开评测。
  • 发现证据距离变远时，判官仍能发现错误，却更难找对证据。记忆探针表明问题主要在于没有回头查证，而非上下文容量不足；强制引用证据可缓解这一问题，说明评测不能只看检出率，还需衡量证据回溯能力。
CultMetric: Gloss-Grounded Cultural Fidelity Evaluation for Machine Translation 2026

Co-first author · Manuscript in preparation: planned submission to COLING.共同第一作者 · 论文准备中：计划投稿 COLING。

  • Developed a method to check whether translations preserve cultural meaning. Built a glossary of 872 expert-validated cultural terms and supplied relevant definitions and translations to the LLM judge to help it identify lost or misrepresented meaning.
  • Separated error identification from scoring: the model identifies and explains translation problems, then fixed rules turn those findings into a 0–100 score. This makes scores traceable and limits the tendency to reward fluent translations that lose cultural content.
  • Evaluated four translation systems on Journey to the West; the resulting system ranking matched expert judgment. Tests with paraphrased translations further showed that the method detects loss of cultural meaning even when the text remains fluent.
  • 提出检验译文是否保留原文文化含义的评测方法。构建包含 872 个专家核验词条的文化词表，将相关释义与译法提供给 LLM 判官，帮助其识别文化含义的遗漏与误译。
  • 将找出问题与计算分数分开：模型先指出并解释翻译问题，再由固定规则转换为 0–100 分，使评分依据可追溯，减少“译文流畅但文化含义丢失”仍获高分的偏差。
  • 在《西游记》上评测四个翻译系统，得到与专家一致的系统排名；通过改写译文的实验，进一步验证该方法能识别语言依然流畅、文化含义却被削弱的情况。
