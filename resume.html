DeepResearch RewardBench: Reward-Model Benchmark for Deep-Research Reports 2025 – 2026

Tech Stack:技术栈: Python, PyTorch, vLLM, Hugging Face Transformers

  • Built the first reward-model benchmark for long-form deep-research reports, addressing a gap in existing LLM evaluation.
  • Designed an end-to-end data pipeline spanning 19 agent/LLM families; combined automatic filtering with PhD-level human verification to construct pair, triplet, and quadruplet preference data comprising 331 tasks and 866 reports.
  • Evaluated 17 models from 7B to 235B with Best-of-N selection and full-ranking consistency metrics; analyzed URL robustness, input-order bias, and comparative versus pointwise scoring.
  • Developed a four-category judge-error taxonomy and a few-shot classifier reaching ~87% agreement with human annotation.
  • 构建首个面向长篇深度研究报告的奖励模型评测基准,填补现有大语言模型评测在该场景的空白。
  • 设计覆盖 19 个智能体/大模型家族的端到端数据流水线,结合自动过滤与博士级人工校验,构建包含 331 项任务、866 份报告的 pair、triplet 与 quadruplet 偏好数据。
  • 基于 Best-of-N 选择与全排序一致性指标评测 17 个 7B–235B 模型,并分析 URL 鲁棒性、输入顺序偏差及对比式与打分式范式。
  • 构建四类裁判错误体系及 few-shot 自动分类器,与人工标注一致率约 87%。
CultMetric: Cultural-Fidelity Evaluation for Classical Chinese Translation 2026

Tech Stack:技术栈: Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, SciPy

  • Proposed a human-free neuro-symbolic evaluation framework grounded in 872 culture-specific items, with five interpretable pillars aggregated into deterministic and reproducible scores.
  • Evaluated four machine-translation systems on the first five chapters of Journey to the West, totaling 1,598 segments per system.
  • Validated discriminative power and independence with Wilcoxon and Spearman tests: system-level ranking matched expert judgement exactly, while correlation with five mainstream metrics remained low (|ρ| < 0.15).
  • 提出以 872 条文化专有项为知识底座的无人工神经符号评测框架,通过五项可解释指标生成确定且可复现的评分。
  • 在《西游记》前五回上评测四个机器翻译系统,每个系统包含 1,598 个句段。
  • 通过 Wilcoxon 与 Spearman 检验验证判别力和独立性:系统级排序与专家完全一致,与五个主流指标的相关性保持较低(|ρ| < 0.15)。
