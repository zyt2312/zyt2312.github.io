DeepResearch RewardBench: Evaluating Reward Models for Deep Research Reports 2025 – 2026

Co-first author · Accepted to Findings of EMNLP 2026.共同第一作者 · 已被 Findings of EMNLP 2026 接收。

  • Built a reward-model benchmark for long-form deep-research reports, with an end-to-end pipeline spanning 19 agent/LLM families; combined automatic filtering and human verification to construct preference data covering 331 tasks and 866 reports.
  • Evaluated 17 models from 7B to 235B with Best-of-N selection and full-ranking consistency metrics; analyzed URL robustness, input-order bias, and comparative versus pointwise scoring.
  • Developed a four-category judge-error taxonomy and a few-shot classifier reaching ~87% agreement with human annotation.
  • 构建面向长篇深度研究报告的奖励模型评测基准，设计覆盖 19 个智能体/大模型家族的端到端流水线，结合自动过滤与人工校验，产出包含 331 项任务、866 份报告的偏好数据。
  • 基于 Best-of-N 选择与全排序一致性指标评测 17 个 7B–235B 模型,并分析 URL 鲁棒性、输入顺序偏差及对比式与打分式范式。
  • 构建四类裁判错误体系及 few-shot 自动分类器,与人工标注一致率约 87%。
LongJudgeBench: Benchmarking LLM Judges on Agent Trajectory Error Localization and Evidence Attribution 2026

First author · Submitted to ACL Rolling Review (October 2026).第一作者 · 已投稿至 ACL Rolling Review（2026 年 10 月）。

  • Introduced a benchmark for three complementary judge capabilities: error detection, error localization, and attribution of supporting execution evidence. It contains 590 natural trajectories (NSet) and 262 constructed error cases (CSet).
  • Constructed CSet with executable interventions and autonomous agent continuation across five error families, providing explicit error locations and supporting evidence references where applicable; evaluated 10 LLM judges.
  • Found a substantial evidence-attribution gap: on CSet, Claude Opus 5.5 reached 94.6% detection accuracy and 98.8% conditional localization accuracy, but only 60.0% Evidence Hit (the highest among evaluated judges).
  • 提出评测 LLM 判官三项能力的基准：在长程智能体轨迹中发现错误、定位错误并归因支持判断的执行证据。包含 590 条自然轨迹（NSet）和 262 个构造错误案例（CSet）。
  • 通过可执行干预与智能体自主续写构造 CSet，覆盖五类错误，并在适用时提供明确的错误位置及支持证据；共评测 10 个 LLM 判官。
  • 实验发现证据归因存在明显短板：Claude Opus 5.5 在 CSet 上的错误检出准确率为 94.6%，条件定位准确率为 98.8%，但 Evidence Hit 仅为 60.0%（在参评判官中最高）。
CIFE: Glossary-Grounded, Item-Level Evaluation of Cultural Fidelity in Literary Machine Translation 2026

Co-first author · Submitted to ACL Rolling Review (October 2026).共同第一作者 · 已投稿至 ACL Rolling Review（2026 年 10 月）。

  • Introduced a reference-free metric that evaluates cultural fidelity at the glossary-item level, so culture-specific items in literary translations are assessed rather than being diluted in a sentence-level score.
  • For each item, an LLM judge assigns an MQM-style severity constrained by the named translation strategy and provides a translation quote that is automatically checked, making verdicts auditable.
  • Evaluated English translations of Journey to the West and a Telugu Ramayana. CIFE's correlation with human cultural-fidelity ratings exceeded the baselines, while experiments also exposed limitations such as penalizing some valid names and paraphrases.
  • 提出一种无需参考译文、基于术语表逐项评估文化忠实度的方法，避免文学翻译中的文化特定项被句子级总分稀释。
  • LLM 判官为每个词条给出受翻译策略约束的 MQM 风格严重度，并提供可自动核验的译文引文，使评测结论可审计。
  • 在《西游记》英文译本和泰卢固语《罗摩衍那》上进行评测。CIFE 与人工文化忠实度评分的相关性高于各基线；实验也揭示其局限，例如可能惩罚部分有效的人名译法与释义改写。
