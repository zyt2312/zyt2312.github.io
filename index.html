§ 02Research Overview研究方向THEMATIC PILLARS研究主题

I.

LLM Reliability & Safety Evaluation大模型可靠性与安全评测

Building rigorous benchmarks to evaluate LLM judges, scoring rubrics, and the reliability of model-generated assessments.

构建严谨的评测基准，衡量 LLM 判官、评分标准及模型生成评价的可靠性。

II.

Trustworthy Agents & Deep Research可信智能体与深度研究

Studying error detection, evidence tracing, and recovery in long-horizon agents and deep research, towards reliable agent training and multi-agent collaboration.

关注长程智能体与深度研究系统的错误检测、证据回溯和自主纠错，探索可靠的智能体训练与多智能体协作。

III.

Reward Modeling & Reward Hacking奖励模型与奖励投机

Evaluating reward-model reliability and robustness, and investigating how agents exploit reward signals and how to detect and mitigate such behavior.

评测奖励模型的判断能力与鲁棒性，研究智能体如何利用奖励机制的漏洞，以及如何识别和缓解这些行为。

I also maintain an active interest in agent hardness, agent skills, and AI watermarking, and closely follow the latest research developments in these emerging areas.

同时对 agent hardness、智能体技能（agent skills）与 AI 水印等前沿方向保持积极兴趣，并持续跟踪相关领域的最新研究进展。

  • DeepResearch RewardBench (co-first author) — built the first reward-model benchmark for long-form deep-research reports: an end-to-end pipeline over 19 agent/LLM families yielding preference datasets of 331 tasks / 866 reports, evaluation of 17 models (7B–235B), and a few-shot judge-error classifier reaching ~87% agreement with humans. Accepted to Findings of EMNLP 2026.
  • CIFE (co-first author) — developed a glossary-grounded, item-level metric for cultural fidelity in literary machine translation. It grades each cultural item with an evidence-checked LLM judgment and was evaluated on English translations of Journey to the West and a Telugu Ramayana; submitted to October 2026 ACL Rolling Review.
  • LongJudgeBench (first author) — built a benchmark of 590 natural agent trajectories and 262 constructed error cases to measure LLM judges' error detection, localization, and evidence attribution; submitted to October 2026 ACL Rolling Review.
  • DeepResearch RewardBench(共同第一作者)—— 构建首个面向长篇深度研究报告的奖励模型评测基准:聚合 19 个智能体/大模型家族的端到端数据流水线,产出 331 任务 / 866 份报告的偏好数据集,系统评测 17 个模型(7B–235B),并提炼出与人工一致率约 87% 的 few-shot 错误分类器。已被 EMNLP 2026 Findings 接收。
  • CIFE(共同第一作者)—— 提出基于术语表、逐项评估文学机器翻译文化忠实度的方法；LLM 判官对每个文化特定项评分并提供可核验的译文证据，在《西游记》英文译本和泰卢固语《罗摩衍那》上进行评测；已投稿至 2026 年 10 月 ACL Rolling Review。
  • LongJudgeBench(第一作者)—— 构建包含 590 条自然智能体轨迹和 262 个构造错误案例的基准，评测 LLM 判官的错误检出、定位与证据归因能力；已投稿至 2026 年 10 月 ACL Rolling Review。

§ 06Selected Projects代表项目WORK项目

LongJudgeBench Submitted · ARR Oct 2026已投稿 · 2026 年 10 月 ARR

Benchmarks three LLM-judge capabilities on agent trajectories: error detection, localization, and attribution of supporting execution evidence. It contains 590 natural trajectories and 262 constructed error cases across five error families; evaluation of 10 judges revealed a substantial evidence-attribution gap.

评测 LLM 判官在智能体轨迹中的三项能力：错误检出、错误定位和支持证据归因。数据集包含 590 条自然轨迹及覆盖五类错误的 262 个构造案例；对 10 个判官的评测发现，证据归因仍存在明显短板。

LLM-as-a-Judge · Agent Evaluation · Evidence Grounding

CIFE Submitted · ARR Oct 2026已投稿 · 2026 年 10 月 ARR

CIFE (Glossary-Grounded, Item-Level Evaluation of Cultural Fidelity in Literary Machine Translation) evaluates cultural items individually. An LLM assigns strategy-constrained severity scores and quotes translation evidence that is automatically checked; evaluated on Journey to the West and a Telugu Ramayana.

CIFE（基于术语表的文学机器翻译文化忠实度逐项评估）逐项评测译文中的文化特定项。LLM 根据翻译策略判定错误严重程度并引用可自动核验的译文证据；评测对象包括《西游记》英文译本和泰卢固语《罗摩衍那》。
